Methodology

A disciplined path from evidence to decision.

Measure the workload, test it against every constraint that matters, and only then commit to a recommendation we can defend.

01 — What we measure

Eight dimensions, one gate.

A single dimension that does not fit is enough to hold.

◱

CPU

Projected fit at p95/p99 on the candidate class.

▤

Memory

Working memory floor, including buffer pool.

⇄

IOPS

Observed physical IOPS vs effective limits.

≋

Throughput

Sustained MiB/s against provisioned ceilings.

◷

tempdb

Placement, version store, and capacity.

◆

Edition

Enterprise-to-Standard eligibility.

▣

Orderability

Exact RDS availability of class and version.

◔

Evidence

Window length and sample continuity.

02 — Reading a decision

Three outcomes, one standard.

A

Recommended

A safe, in-family downsize. Every gate fits at the measured confidence level.

B

Aggressive optimization

A larger, often cross-family move the evidence supports but which warrants review.

C

Stay as is

At least one dimension blocks every smaller candidate. Holding is the evidence-based choice.
