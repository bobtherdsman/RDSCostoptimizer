Optimization guide

How the workload-fit decision is made.

The service turns collector evidence into a defensible business decision. Here is what it measures, the guardrails it enforces, and how to read a result.

At a glance

Method
Evidence-led
Engine
SQL Server only
Window
48h min · 7d+ preferred
Pricing
Deferred

CPU

Underutilized CPU is the primary downsize signal; projected CPU must fit the smaller instance at p95/p99.

Memory

The memory floor (buffer pool + working set) must fit before recommending a smaller class.

I/O & throughput

Observed physical IOPS and throughput must fit the candidate's effective limits.

tempdb

tempdb placement and capacity are assessed as part of the fit and top-driver view.

Edition & orderability

Enterprise→Standard eligibility and exact RDS orderability are checked for the target.

Evidence quality

Window length and sample continuity set the confidence level of the result.

Reading a result

Three outcomes

Recommended

A safe like-for-like or in-family downsize; all gates fit.

Aggressive

A larger, cross-family move that fits the evidence but warrants review.

Stay as is

At least one dimension blocks a smaller candidate; keep the current class.

Evidence window

Confidence scales with the window

< 48hInsufficient
48–72hBelow preliminary
3–7 daysPreliminary
7–14 daysMinimum · medium
14–30 daysPreferred · high
30–32 daysMonthly cycle · high
